How to read: Cooler colors indicate slower currents; magenta indicates faster currents. Dark arrows show modeled current direction; arrow length and visual movement are exaggerated for readability, not proportional travel distance. The speed colors use a 0–1.5 knot scale, with higher values capped. Colors are interpolated across NOAA's actual water-only triangular mesh, rather than estimated across land. Nearshore predictions may be inaccurate. Source: NOAA SSCOFS FVCOM, user-provided subset (Nov 8–9 UTC). Base map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
