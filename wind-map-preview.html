Wind field is sampled directly from NOAA SSCOFS marine mesh cells and displayed as colored markers with animated directional arrows. Arrow travel is an illustration of direction, not particle trajectories or predicted wave movement. Wind direction in readouts is meteorological “from” true north. No gust or wave-height estimates. This is a research preview, not an independently validated local forecast, navigational aid, or safety clearance. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
